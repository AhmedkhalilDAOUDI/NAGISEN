# Third-party notices

## Open Food Facts Morocco catalog

The bundled `MoroccanFoods.json` is derived from community-contributed product data at
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/country/morocco under the Open Database License (ODbL).
It contains products listed for Morocco with 611-prefix barcodes and complete calorie
and macro values. Product labels should be checked before use.

License and attribution: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/data

## Open Prices

Barcode-linked community price observations are read from Open Prices by Open Food Facts:
https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/

The data is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL). Observations may be old or
incomplete, so NAGISEN shows their dates and asks users to confirm current prices.

License and data information: https://openfoodfacts.github.io/open-prices/guides/data/
