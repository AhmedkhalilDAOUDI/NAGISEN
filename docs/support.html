Common questions

How do I put NAGISEN on my Home Screen?

On iPhone, open NAGISEN in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app. It opens full screen and works offline, except Feed, food search and the coach.

Does the web version keep my data?

Yes, in your browser on that device. Export my data (in You, from the Dashboard) saves a copy you can import on another device.

How do I change my calorie or macro targets?

Open Goals from your profile on the Dashboard. The targets set on first launch are a starting point; change them whenever you like.

Where is my data, and how do I keep a copy?

Your history lives on your iPhone, with a backup in your private iCloud when you're signed in. Export my data saves a JSON copy wherever you choose. See the privacy policy.

How do I use the coach?

Tap the sparkle button on any screen, then add your DeepSeek API key in Chat settings. Turn on Use my NAGISEN data only if you want the coach to see your day.

How do I report or block someone in Feed?

Tap the "…" on their post, comment or story and choose Report or Block. Blocked members' posts, comments and stories disappear for you, and they aren't told.

How do I delete my Feed account?

Open your profile from the Dashboard, tap your Feed account, then Delete account. Your account, profile and everything you shared are removed for good. Your history on your iPhone stays.
