Progress in stillness

The art of quiet progress.

A calm iPhone companion for what you eat, how you train and the habits between them.

NAGISEN is in development and not on the App Store yet.

  • Food, without the noise Log meals by search, barcode, a photo of the package, voice or by hand, with calories, protein, carbs and fat for each day.
  • Targets that fit you A few questions on first launch set your calorie, macro and water targets with the Mifflin–St Jeor estimate. You can change them any time.
  • Water, caffeine and cost Track water, see how much caffeine is still with you at bedtime, and keep the prices of the foods you buy.
  • Training Personal records, a weekly split, daily side quests and trophies, with steps, workouts and weight from Apple Health.
  • A streak worth keeping Check off the day when you're done logging and watch the week fill in.
  • Feed An optional community: share photos, videos and stories, follow people, like and comment.
  • A coach, everywhere Ask NAGISEN AI anything from any screen. It uses your own DeepSeek key, and only sees your day if you allow it.
