NAGISEN Privacy Support

Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

What NAGISEN keeps on your iPhone, what it sends, and to whom.

Effective October 31, 2026.

NAGISEN has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not sell or share your data. Nothing below is sent unless you use the feature it belongs to.

On your iPhone

Your food diary, goals, setup answers, weight, water, caffeine, workouts, records, prices, streaks and coach conversations are stored on your iPhone.

iCloud backup

When you're signed in to iCloud, NAGISEN keeps a backup of that history in your private iCloud database. Only your Apple Account can read it; we can't.

Exports

Export my data creates a JSON file you choose where to save. Lose It! imports read the file you pick on your iPhone.

Apple Health

With your permission, NAGISEN reads your steps, activity, heart rate, body weight and workouts to show your progress, and saves workouts you start in the app to Apple Health. Health data stays on your iPhone. The one exception: if you turn on Use my NAGISEN data in the coach, today's step count is part of the summary sent with your question (see the coach section below).

Camera, microphone and speech

Barcodes, food packages and receipts are read on your iPhone. Voice search is transcribed on your iPhone. Photos and recordings are not uploaded for these features.

Food and price lookups

  • Food searches and scanned barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts to find nutrition information.
  • When you check a scanned product's price, its barcode is sent to Open Prices.
  • In Morocco, the product name may be looked up on MyMarket (mymarket.ma), an online shop, for a shelf price.
  • If you've added a DeepSeek key, the product's name, brand and barcode can be sent to DeepSeek for a price estimate.

NAGISEN AI coach

The coach uses your own DeepSeek API key, stored in your iPhone's Keychain. Messages you send go to DeepSeek to be answered. If you turn on Use my NAGISEN data, each message also includes a short summary of today's food, goals, workouts and step count. It's off until you turn it on. Chat history stays on your iPhone.

Feed

Feed is optional and needs an account. It is hosted on Supabase. When you use it, we store:

  • your email address and sign-in details;
  • your username, bio, profile picture and the border you've earned;
  • the posts, photos, videos, stories and comments you share, and your likes and follows;
  • who viewed your stories, and the reports and blocks you make.

What you share is visible to signed-in NAGISEN members. Stories disappear after 24 hours. Your nutrition and workout history is never part of Feed unless you post it yourself.

Deleting your account from your account settings removes your account, profile, posts, comments and shared photos and videos for good. Your history on your iPhone is not affected.

Music

If you connect Spotify, NAGISEN talks to the Spotify app on your iPhone to show what's playing while you train.

Children

NAGISEN is not meant for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
